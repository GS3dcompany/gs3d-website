Advanced 3D Printing Technologies we Offer

We utilize multiple 3D printing technologies to meet diverse project needs—whether it’s high-detail prototypes, functional parts, or large-scale models.

SLA (Stereolithography)

Delivers smooth, high-resolution parts using liquid resin cured by UV light. Ideal for prototypes, intricate detailing, and presentation models.

FDM (Fused Deposition Modeling)

A cost-effective method where parts are built layer by layer using thermoplastic filaments. Perfect for functional prototypes and end-use components.

SLS (Selective Laser Sintering)

Uses a laser to fuse powdered material, producing strong, durable, and complex geometries without requiring support structures.
